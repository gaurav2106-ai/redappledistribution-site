Carriers

We ship with FedEx and UPS. Orders go to US addresses only.

Minimum order

Bulk quotes start at 10 boxes, as we are a wholesaler. Under 10 boxes? Send the request anyway and our team will tell you what is possible.

Credits

We do not offer replacements. If your order arrives damaged or you receive a duplicate product, we will issue a credit after we verify the issue with the shipping partner and the vendor. Otherwise, no credit is issued.
