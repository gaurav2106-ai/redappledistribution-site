What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms: your name, shop name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, state and the list of products you want quoted, plus any notes, messages or review text you choose to add. If you create an optional account, see Optional accounts below.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your quote request or message. If you sign up for updates, we use your email or WhatsApp number to send the new drops and restock alerts you asked for. Reviews are shown on the site only after we approve them, with the name and shop you gave.

How forms are delivered

Our forms, including account sign-ups, send your submission to our team by email through the FormSubmit email service. This means your submission passes through FormSubmit on its way to us, and FormSubmit has its own privacy practices.

Optional accounts

Accounts are optional. You can browse and request quotes without one. If you create an account we keep what you enter on the sign-up form (name, shop name, email, phone, WhatsApp number, state, what you are looking for and your preferred payment method), your saved quote list and the quote requests you send while signed in. The payment method is a preference only. We never ask for card or bank numbers on this website.

Account data is stored with our account provider, Supabase, so you can sign in from any device. Your password is handled by the provider and we do not see it. Our team can see your account details, reviews new accounts, and gets an email with your sign-up details. To correct or delete your account, contact us: sales@redappledistro.com, (347) 937-9103 or WhatsApp +1 (502) 616-3671. Business hours are 8:30 AM to 9:30 PM US time.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your information.

Your browser

To make the site work, your browser keeps your 21+ confirmation and your quote list on your own device. If you sign in to an account, it also keeps your sign-in there until you sign out.

Cookies and notifications

Essential: the site stores a small amount of data in your browser (local storage and one first-party cookie) to remember that you confirmed you are 21 or older, to keep your quote list, to keep you signed in if you have an account, and to remember your cookie choice (cookie name ra_consent, kept for 180 days). The site cannot work without these.

Optional: analytics and notifications are off unless you allow them. We do not run any analytics tools today, and we do not set optional cookies before you choose. If you allow notifications, we may send you web alerts about new drops, restocks and trending picks on that device. Alerts are delivered through OneSignal, a third-party notification provider, which receives a device token and the topic of what you viewed (for example the last product category) so we can send relevant alerts, but no name, email or other personal details from us. Its script loads only after you allow notifications. Your browser asks for its own permission only after you tap Enable alerts, and we never send alerts without that.

Withdraw any time: open Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page) and switch an option off. Turning notifications off removes the alert registration from your device.
