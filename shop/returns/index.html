About us

Wholesale, made easy for smoke shops.

Red Apple Distribution is a wholesale supplier for smoke shops, vape shops, convenience stores and other retailers across the United States.

Who we are

Red Apple Distribution supplies retailers with the products their customers ask for. Our catalog covers wholesale vapes, flower, hydroxy products, edibles, pre-rolls, kratom, shrooms and smoke accessories, all from brands shop owners already know and trust.

We built Red Apple Distribution around one idea: buying wholesale should be simple. Browse the catalog, build your list, and request a quote. Our team follows up personally with bulk pricing for your shop.

Our goal

Our goal is to give retailers genuine products, honest bulk pricing and service they can count on. Every product we carry is authentic. We do not deal in dupes, duplicates or lookalikes, because your customers and your reputation depend on it.

We ship across the United States with FedEx and UPS, so wherever your shop is, we can supply it. Have a question before you order? Contact our team and we will help.

Why retailers choose us

Quality

Genuine products only, sourced from trusted brands. What you order is what you stock.

Variety

One supplier for many categories. Browse all brands or jump straight to new arrivals and trending products.

Service

A real person follows up on every quote request by WhatsApp, phone or email, whichever you prefer.

Consistency

Reliable supply and clear communication, so you can keep your shelves stocked with confidence.

About us

Wholesale, made easy for smoke shops.

Red Apple Distribution is a wholesale supplier for smoke shops, vape shops, convenience stores and other retailers across the United States.

Who we are

Red Apple Distribution supplies retailers with the products their customers ask for. Our catalog covers wholesale vapes, flower, hydroxy products, edibles, pre-rolls, kratom, shrooms and smoke accessories, all from brands shop owners already know and trust.

We built Red Apple Distribution around one idea: buying wholesale should be simple. Browse the catalog, build your list, and request a quote. Our team follows up personally with bulk pricing for your shop.

Our goal

Our goal is to give retailers genuine products, honest bulk pricing and service they can count on. Every product we carry is authentic. We do not deal in dupes, duplicates or lookalikes, because your customers and your reputation depend on it.

We ship across the United States with FedEx and UPS, so wherever your shop is, we can supply it. Have a question before you order? Contact our team and we will help.

Why retailers choose us

Quality

Genuine products only, sourced from trusted brands. What you order is what you stock.

Variety

One supplier for many categories. Browse all brands or jump straight to new arrivals and trending products.

Service

A real person follows up on every quote request by WhatsApp, phone or email, whichever you prefer.

Consistency

Reliable supply and clear communication, so you can keep your shelves stocked with confidence.

Red Apple Distribution sells wholesale only, to licensed retailers in the United States. This website is for adults 21 and older who are buying for a retail business. By using it you confirm that you are 21 or older and that you are requesting a quote for a retail business.

Bulk quotes start at 10 boxes. Under 10 boxes? Send the request anyway and our team will tell you what is possible.

Pricing

Local laws

Availability, legality and labeling rules differ by state and locality. The buyer is responsible for knowing and following all laws that apply where they sell.

Red Apple Distribution sells wholesale only, to licensed retailers in the United States. This website is for adults 21 and older who are buying for a retail business. By using it you confirm that you are 21 or older and that you are requesting a quote for a retail business.

Quotes, not online orders

This website is quote-only. Nothing is charged on this website, and sending a quote request is not a purchase. Our team contacts you with bulk pricing and next steps.

Bulk quotes start at 10 boxes. Under 10 boxes? Send the request anyway and our team will tell you what is possible.

Pricing

Pricing is provided per quote and depends on the products, the quantity and your state.

Shipping

We ship with FedEx and UPS to US addresses only. See Shipping and delivery for details.

Damaged or duplicate product

We do not offer replacements. If your order arrives damaged or you receive a duplicate product, we will issue a credit after we verify the issue with the shipping partner and the vendor. Otherwise, no credit is issued. See Returns and credits.

Local laws

Availability, legality and labeling rules differ by state and locality. The buyer is responsible for knowing and following all laws that apply where they sell.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

On this page

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms: your name, shop name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, state and the list of products you want quoted, plus any notes, messages or review text you choose to add.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your quote request or message. If you sign up for updates, we use your email or WhatsApp number to send the new drops and restock alerts you asked for. Reviews are shown on the site only after we approve them, with the name and shop you gave.

How forms are delivered

Our forms send your submission to our team by email through the FormSubmit email service. This means your submission passes through FormSubmit on its way to us, and FormSubmit has its own privacy practices.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your information.

Your browser

To make the site work, your browser keeps your 21+ confirmation and your quote list on your own device.

Cookies and notifications

Essential: the site stores a small amount of data in your browser (local storage and one first-party cookie) to remember that you confirmed you are 21 or older, to keep your quote list, and to remember your cookie choice (cookie name ra_consent, kept for 180 days). The site cannot work without these.

Optional: analytics and notifications are off unless you allow them. We do not run any analytics tools today, and we do not set optional cookies before you choose. If you allow notifications, we may send you web alerts about new drops, restocks and trending picks on that device. Alerts are delivered through OneSignal, a third-party notification provider, which receives a device token and the topic of what you viewed (for example the last product category) so we can send relevant alerts, but no name, email or other personal details from us. Its script loads only after you allow notifications. Your browser asks for its own permission only after you tap Enable alerts, and we never send alerts without that.

Withdraw any time: open Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page) and switch an option off. Turning notifications off removes the alert registration from your device.

Contact us

Questions about your information? Reach our team at sales@redappledistro.com, (347) 937-9103 or WhatsApp +1 (502) 616-3671. Business hours are 8:30 AM to 9:30 PM US time.
